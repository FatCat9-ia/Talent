Metric definitions and delivery rules
Relevant posts
Public posts kept after relevance, wording-dictionary and noise filtering. The base for every rate.
Share of conversation
A talent's relevant posts divided by all monitored talent's posts, across every studio, in the same month, market and platform. Not a ranking against all celebrities.
Talent-pull rate
Posts saying the performer motivates watching or interest in a title, divided by relevant posts. A public signal, not viewing behavior.
Title association
Posts that explicitly link the talent to their monitored title or character. Measures title transfer rather than fame.
Intent
Wants to watch, recommends, fan / advocacy, fatigue / rejection, and other discussion (mostly performance talk). Together they make 100%.
Engagement / 1K views
Likes + comments + shares divided by public views, × 1,000, only where the platform shows views.
Authors
Distinct public authors per platform. Summed across platforms, so the same person may count more than once.
Event lift
Change in conversation in an event window versus the baseline. Directional, not paid-media attribution.
Sample status
Cells under the minimum sample are flagged and greyed out rather than reported.
Scope
Public data only, monthly delivery, country-level results. This demo uses simulated data for 16 current talents across 7 studios, 5 markets, 7 platforms and 6 months. Events are illustrative.
